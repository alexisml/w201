Post #6 by John5788 (from an FAE catalog): the 3-pin switch comes in two kinds. 006 545 42 24 (red, 100–95 °C / 107–97 °C, "models with A/C") and 006 545 45 24 (green, 105–100 °C / 115–105 °C) are dual thermoswitches, as is the gray 006 545 61 24 (105–100 °C / 120–115 °C). The black 006 545 91 24 is a 100 °C switch plus a thermistor for the gauge. Post #2 (Mxfrank, a poster's view): the red switch's first circuit (about 100 °C) runs the electric fan at high speed, the second (about 105 °C) turns off the A/C compressor; 008 542 45 17 and 005 542 10 17 are other sensors shown for comparison.
