# 1992 190E 2.3 Temp/Fan Switch/Sensor Questions (PeachParts forum, 2015)

- **Wayback Machine:** <https://web.archive.org/web/20170521002233/http://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/364526-1992-190e-2-3-temp-fan-switch-sensor-questions.html> (all 8 posts; the attachments are not in it)

- **Archived:** 2026-10-11

A 1992 190 E 2.3 with A/C: the black-top 3-pin switch on the cylinder head uses 2 pins for the fan clutch and the 3rd for the temperature gauge, and locked the fan at 110 °C. The poster quotes the service manual: M102 cars with A/C use a 3-pin 110 °C switch, cars without A/C a 2-pin 100 °C switch, and on A/C cars the auxiliary fan runs whenever the fan clutch is engaged, through a "double-contact relay which is activated by the triple temperature switch". A new 006 545 91 24 (same as the old one) still locked at 110 °C; a 006 545 42 24 from an 85 190 D is a pure switch (3rd pin is a switch, not a gauge sender). A light blue 2-pin switch next to it ran the aux fan on high speed (seen at 130 °C); another poster points to the A/C receiver-drier pressure switch for the aux fan. Not resolved in the thread.
