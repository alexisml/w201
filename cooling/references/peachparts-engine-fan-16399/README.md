# ENGINE FAN (PeachParts forum, 2001–2003)

- **Original:** <https://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/16399-engine-fan.html> (page 2: <https://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/16399-engine-fan-2.html>)

- **Archived:** 2026-10-11

On the 4-cylinder 190 E the engine fan is driven by an electromagnetic clutch on the water pump, switched by a coolant temperature switch that grounds the clutch circuit (switch on at about 100 °C, per the posters; one says 105 °C). The clutch has a connector right on the coil; a 1991 190 E 1.8 owner traced a blowing fuse #10 (tach) to a shorted clutch coil.
