About a 1986 190 E 2.3-16 (not our 8V). The opening post asks whether the fan clutch relay is the silver relay in the separate box in front of the main fuse box (not answered directly); the poster later replaced it as "the k-8 relay in the fuse panel". Others say shorting the 2-pin fan clutch temperature switch connector (engine off, ignition at position II) should click the relay and lock the fan. On that car one switch terminal has 12 V and the other (two wires) goes to the clutch circuit; the poster's fault was not resolved in the thread.
