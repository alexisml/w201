Post #2 by slk230red (1988 190 E 2.3): the pair of contacts on the 3-prong temperature switch behind the thermostat drives the fan clutch (short the 2-wire connector, ignition on, and the magnet engages); the 2-prong switch behind it drives the aux fan relay. With A/C on the fans also run from the high-pressure switch on the receiver/drier; the fan clutch switch is 100 °C or 110 °C depending on the part.
