# 190e location of sensor for electromagnetic fan (PeachParts forum, 2008)

- **Original:** <https://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/220289-190e-location-sensor-electromagnetic-fan.html>
- **Wayback Machine:** no snapshot found (2026-10-11)
- **Full copy:** kept in the private archive (PDF of the thread, 4 posts, no pictures). Available on request.
- **Archived:** 2026-10-11

On a 1986 190 E 2.3 8V the fan clutch is switched by the 3-pin red or green temperature switch in the coolant manifold at the front of the engine. Shorting its connector with the ignition on should make the clutch engage audibly.
