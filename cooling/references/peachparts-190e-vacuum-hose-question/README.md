# 190E Vacuum hose question w/pic (PeachParts forum, 2005)

- **Archived:** 2026-10-11

A 1993 190E 2.3 owner finds an unconnected small vacuum hose on the intake manifold. Outcome: the port on the upper side of intake runner #3 is for the transmission vacuum modulator on the driver's side of the transmission (only one hose goes to it); the ports on the underside of the manifold go to the thermo-vacuum valves. Another owner advises capping an open nipple so it does not leak until the valve is replaced.
