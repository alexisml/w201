# Interior restoration (planned)

**System:** interior
**Status:** planned

Plan to restore the door panels, carpets and other interior details. This file collects parts and
sources for that job. Part numbers come from EPC catalog 452 (190 E 2.3 USA, 1988), via the private
parts catalog (available on request).

Interior on this car: **MB-Tex, Medium Red** (code 177), from the
[data card](../general-reference/data-card.md).

The catalog doesn't list separate clips for the panel edges. Sellers sell `A 201 988 11 78` as the
W201 door panel clip (seller info, not confirmed in the catalog). Old clips usually break when a
panel comes off, so buy spares.
