# Engine bay (planned)

**System:** engine
**Status:** planned

Cosmetic parts seen with the hood open. Part numbers come from EPC catalog 452 (190 E 2.3 USA,
1988), via the private parts catalog (available on request).

## Ignition cable cover (on the cylinder head cover)

EPC 15.060 Ignition System. Two long pieces clip together over the spark plug cables on top of the
cylinder head cover. A short end piece guides the cables and the breather line.

- [ ] **Ignition cable cover, top**: `A 102 159 11 40` (pos 176). The part you see with the hood open
- [ ] **Ignition cable holder, bottom**: `A 102 159 12 40` (pos 179). The base the cables sit in
- [ ] **End guide, cables and breather line**: `A 102 159 19 40` (pos 185)

The catalog names all three "bracket, ignition cables" and lists no replacement numbers.

Fitting notes:
- w201.com owners confirm the three numbers: `11 40` is the top of the cable rail, `12 40` the
  bottom part that clips in from below, `19 40` the front holder. Order: clip in the bottom rail,
  lay the cables in, clip on the cover, then slide the assembly forward under the front holder. A
  little silicone spray helps; the old plastic breaks easily. Replace both rail halves with the
  leads ([Zündkabelwechsel](https://w201.com/index.php?thread/89293-z%C3%BCndkabelwechsel/),
  [reference](references/w201com-zuendkabelwechsel/README.md)).
- The cover is only pushed on. Take off the front piece (with the hose holder) first, then the long
  cover. Don't run the leads loose without it
  ([Ventildeckel/ Abdeckung Zündkabel](https://w201-ev.de/forum/thread/6091-ventildeckel-abdeckung-z%C3%BCndkabel/),
  [reference](references/w201ev-ventildeckel-abdeckung-zuendkabel/README.md)).
- Step-by-step photos for 201.028: AUTODOC Club "How to change ignition leads on Mercedes W201"
  removes the intake pipe (steps 4–6) and the cable bracket (step 7)
  ([guide](https://club.autodoc.co.uk/manuals/how-to-change-ignition-leads-on-mercedes-w201-replacement-guide-35455),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20260211090339/https://club.autodoc.co.uk/manuals/how-to-change-ignition-leads-on-mercedes-w201-replacement-guide-35455);
  [PDF](https://club.autodoc.co.uk/pdf-manuals/club/mercedes-benz/m190/ignition-lead/pdf/EN-how-to-change-ignition-leads-on-mercedes-w201-replacement-guide.pdf),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20240716095715/https://club.autodoc.co.uk/pdf-manuals/club/mercedes-benz/m190/ignition-lead/pdf/EN-how-to-change-ignition-leads-on-mercedes-w201-replacement-guide.pdf)).

## Air intake hose (to the oval air cleaner)

EPC 09.015 Air Cleaner. Ribbed hose into the inlet of the oval air cleaner housing
(`A 015 094 88 02`, confirmed on this car).

- [ ] **Hose**: **`A 102 094 56 82`** (pos 80), confirmed for this car. It connects the intake
  scoop next to the radiator to the air cleaner. Reproduction sold by
  [rpm-depot](https://rpm-depot.de/en/product/air-filter-intake-hose-m102-8v-mercedes-w201-190e-up-to-9-1988-a1020945682/)
  ([reference](references/rpm-depot-m102-intake-hose/README.md)), listed for M102.985 KE-Jetronic,
  up to 9/1988. The other hose at pos 80, `A 102 094 17 87`, is not the one for this car
- [ ] *Alternative:* [rpm-depot "Upgrade intake" kit](https://rpm-depot.de/en/product/upgrade-intake-mercedes-w201-190e-1-8-2-0-2-3-m102/)
  (aftermarket scoop, hose and fastening parts). The listing says it fits "M102 models with small
  radiator", so check that it fits this car first

Notes:
- The genuine hose is listed as **discontinued** at MB Parts Giant
  ([page](https://www.mbpartsgiant.com/genuine/mercedes-benz~hose~1020945682),
  [reference](references/mbpartsgiant-1020945682/README.md)), so the reproduction may be the only
  new option.
- The scoop and hose bring cooler air from in front of the radiator to the filter box. Make sure the
  barbs on the scoop catch the lip of the hose, or it keeps coming apart; liquid soap helps
  ([Missing air inlet](https://www.benzworld.org/threads/missing-air-inlet.555365/),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20230202210345/https://www.benzworld.org/threads/missing-air-inlet.555365/)).
- Some owners leave it off when a non-original radiator is in the way; one reply says the effect is
  small ([Missing air intake](https://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/91260-missing-air-intake.html),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20200921031406/http://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/91260-missing-air-intake.html)).
