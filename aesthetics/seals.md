# Hood and engine bay seals (planned)

**System:** body
**Status:** planned

Rubber seals around the hood. Part numbers come from EPC catalog 452 (190 E 2.3 USA, 1988), via
the private parts catalog (available on request).

## Seal at the rear of the engine bay (below the hood)

EPC 68.055 Insulation in engine compartment. U-channel edge seal that slips over the edge of the
panel at the rear of the engine bay, where the hood closes against the fire wall.

- [ ] **Seal, partition panel to fire wall**: `A 124 628 52 98` (pos 32), 1715 mm.
  **Order `A 210 628 00 98` instead.** A supplier search showed "use 210-628-00-98 instead"
  (the replacement doesn't appear in EPC 452)

Notes:
- MB Parts Giant names `A 210 628 00 98` "Partition Panel Seal" and says it replaces
  `124-628-52-98`. Its fitment list shows W124/W126 models, not the 190E, and no length
  ([page](https://www.mbpartsgiant.com/genuine/mercedes-benz~seal~2106280098),
  [reference](references/mbpartsgiant-2106280098/README.md)).
- Position: step 3 of a PeachParts W201 blower motor write-up lifts the rubber seals off the top of
  the fire wall (battery and fuse box bays) before the panel comes out; they simply pull off
  ([W201 Blower Motor Removal/Installation](https://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/242345-w201-blower-motor-removal-installation-large-pics.html),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20230130171724/https://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/242345-w201-blower-motor-removal-installation-large-pics.html)).

## Hood side edge seals

EPC 88.120 Engine hood. Rubber seal along the side edge where the hood meets the fender (on the
inner fender edge, see notes below). The catalog calls it "seal at engine hood; for protection of electronic devices
behind battery".

- [ ] **Seal**: `A 201 889 05 98` (pos 156). Replaces `A 124 889 04 98`. The catalog lists **qty 1**
  for this car (the battery side)
- Reproduction set from [MBZ Classic Parts](https://mbzclassicparts.com/en/collections/w-201-sedan-abdichtungen-neu/products/w124-w201-abdichtungs-set-kotfluegel-neu-a2018890598)
  ([reference](references/mbzclassicparts-hood-seal-set/README.md)): sold as a "fender sealing set",
  2 pieces of 50 cm (left and right), for W124 and W201. The second piece would go on the other side,
  where the catalog lists none for this car

Notes:
- **Position to check:** this note first said the seal sits on the hood underside. A BenzWorld W124
  thread shows `A 201 889 05 98` on the **inner fender edge**, in front of the reservoirs, and gives
  it for both sides: left 405 mm, right 440 mm, "to be adapted during assembly". It also says there
  is no strip behind the fire wall because the hood hinge is there
  ([engine hood insulation problem](https://www.benzworld.org/threads/engine-hood-insulation-problem-water-leaking-inside.2816482/),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20230324002819/https://www.benzworld.org/threads/engine-hood-insulation-problem-water-leaking-inside.2816482/)).
  The MBZ photo also shows it along the fender edge. Check where it goes on this car.
