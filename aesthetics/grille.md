## Removal and fitting

1. Open the hood. Undo the cross-head screw at the bottom of the chrome shell (pos 14).
2. Undo the 6 hex screws holding the shell to the hood (pos 59): 4 at the top, and 2 hard-to-reach
   ones in the centre, done with an **8 mm socket on a 1/4" drive and an 8–9" extension**.
3. Remove the laurel-wreath badge (it usually breaks) and the hood star.
4. Pull the spring clips that hold the plastic insert in the shell, and lift it out.

Sources: [Removing 190E Grille? HOWTO, MBClub UK](https://forums.mbclub.co.uk/threads/removing-190e-grille-howto.26817/)
([reference](references/mbclub-removing-190e-grille/README.md));

## What owners do (and the replica problem)

- **Cheap complete replicas (eBay, unbranded) are the problem.** Reports: a replica 1/4–1/2 in. too
  short on each side, so only the 2 centre screws lined up; paint chipping within weeks; chrome
  piece fit "horrible"; 45 minutes of modifying to fit an insert
  (["Aftermarket" 190E Parts?, BenzWorld 2013](https://benzworld.org/forums/w201-190-class/1721564-aftermarket-190e-parts.html),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20180303041935/http://www.benzworld.org/forums/w201-190-class/1721564-aftermarket-190e-parts.html)).
  An €85 complete grille was sent back in 2025: holes off centre, chrome defects, scratched in the
  box ([Kühlergrill, Chromleisten, Inlay, w201.com](https://w201.com/index.php?thread/95866-k%C3%BChlergrill-chromleisten-inlay/),
  [reference](references/w201com-kuehlergrill-chromleisten-inlay/README.md)).
- **The usual advice: keep or buy a good used genuine shell and refurbish it.** Wax the inside of the
  chrome frame with cavity wax, polish the outside, clean the strips; replace only the plastic insert
  (w201.com 95866). Several owners say a good used frame beats any copy (w201.com 95327).
- **For the insert, Van Wezel is the brand owners name as good;** Valeo and Blic (under €15) also
  reported fine. One owner found a genuine MB insert poorly deburred, so fitting the strips was hard;
  another threw away a different brand's insert as "completely wrong" (w201.com 95327, 95866).
  Owners disagree on whether genuine inserts were better.
- **Faded insert?** Inserts fade from dark grey to light grey in the sun; a heat gun doesn't restore
  them. Owners paint them matt grey or use Plasti Dip
  ([Grille black or "silver" plastic??, PeachParts](https://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/288746-grille-black-silver-plastic.html),
  [reference](references/peachparts-grille-black-silver/README.md); w201.com 95327).
- **Branded complete grilles** cross-referenced to `A 201 880 07 83` (autodoc UK, 2026-10-06):
  Diederichs 1620040 (£77), Schlieckmann 140324 (£67), Klokkerholm 3511995 (£72), Van Wezel 3020518
  (£102, chrome, with trim strips), Prasco ME0212000 (£119), Blic 6502-07-3511995P (£120), Trucktec
  02.60.152 (£123), Jumasa 21102016 (£138)
  ([autodoc](https://www.autodoc.co.uk/car-parts/oem/a2018800783), [reference](references/autodoc-a2018800783/README.md)).
  Only Van Wezel has owner feedback ("fine"); the others are untested by the sources found.
- **This car's current grille is not original:** it is one of the cheap copies (owner, 2026-10-06).
- **Shell construction (owner, 2026-10-06):** the outer chrome surround is **metal**; the inner
  part (the insert) is **plastic**.

## Making a cheap replica usable

General advice, not from the sources above. The weak points are fit, chrome and the plastic:

- **Fit:** offer it up to the hood before anything else. If holes are off, slot them with a round
  file rather than forcing screws; a too-short shell can't really be fixed.
- **Fasteners:** throw away the supplied clips and screws and use the genuine ones (still sold).
- **Chrome strips:** swap the replica's strips for genuine ones; they are cheap and the replica
  strips are usually the first thing to pit.
- **Chrome surround:** thin replica chrome can't be made good cheaply. Wax or seal it from new
  (inside too, with cavity wax) to slow corrosion; re-plating costs more than a good used genuine
  shell.
- **Insert:** if it's shiny or the wrong grey, scuff it, use plastic adhesion promoter and paint it
  satin/matt dark grey with a UV-stable paint.
- **Best combination:** genuine metal shell (used, refurbished) + replica insert (Van Wezel) +
  genuine strips and clips. That puts the replica only where it is cheapest to fix or replace.

## Finding a used genuine shell

- One shell number, `A 201 880 07 83`, fits every W201 from 1984 to 1993 (all engines, pre- and
  post-facelift, US and Euro), so any W201 breaker is a candidate. Only the **metal shell** is
  needed; insert, strips and clips can be new.
- Where to look: Ovoko and B-Parts (EU dismantlers), eBay.de and Kleinanzeigen.de (search
  "Kühlergrill W201" or "2018800783"), the sale sections of w201.com and w201-ev.de, and the stores
  in [parts-suppliers.md](../general-reference/parts-suppliers.md).
- A shell with a damaged insert or missing strips is fine; check the chrome surround for pitting,
  dents and broken screw mounts.

## Recommendation

1. Keep the current shell if it's sound, or find a good used genuine one; polish it and wax the inside.
2. New insert: Van Wezel (or genuine if found), plus new clips `A 201 988 25 78` ×12 and
   `A 003 988 20 78` ×3, locks `A 201 994 04 45` ×2 and `05 45` ×2, `A 126 994 12 45` ×4.
3. New genuine chrome strips if the old ones are pitted (still sold).
4. New screws `N 914031 005208` ×6 and seal `A 201 888 00 97`.
5. If buying a complete new grille, prefer Van Wezel over unbranded copies, and check that the 6
   screw holes line up before painting or fitting.
