# Sunroof side wind deflectors (planned)

**System:** body
**Status:** planned

Part numbers come from EPC catalog 452 (190 E 2.3 USA, 1988), via the private parts catalog
(available on request).

## Parts

EPC 78.075 Sliding/lifting roof plate (electric sunroof, option 412). Side covers riveted along the
left and right edges of the sunroof panel. They cover the side gaps when the roof is tilted up.
The catalog calls them "ornamental cover".

- [ ] **Side deflector, left**: `A 140 780 03 92` (pos 47). Replaces `A 126 780 07 92`
- [ ] **Side deflector, right**: `A 140 780 04 92` (pos 47). Replaces `A 126 780 08 92`
- [ ] **Rivets**: `N 910 001 00 3201` (pos 50), 4 needed

## Removing the sunroof panel

Researched, not yet done on this car. The best guide found is for the W124, which uses the same
power tilt sunroof (see the last note below). Where the W124 bolt positions differ from the W201's
is not confirmed.

1. **Interior trim panel**: roll the roof about 3/4 open. Pop the 4 plastic clips along the front
   edge with a large flat screwdriver (insert next to the clip and twist). There are no clips at the
   back. Roll the roof fully open, pull the panel down and forward to clear, then lift it out
   through the roof opening. The headliner stays in place. A 1988 190E 2.3 owner whose roof jammed
   barely open pulled the panel down into the car instead; its thin metal frame bent and was bent
   back ([Sunroof, PeachParts forum, 2001](https://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/16069-sunroof.html),
   [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20250322033640/http://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/16069-sunroof.html)).
2. **Side trims**: remove the 4 screws per side holding the top stainless trim plates. The front
   screw is a machined one.
3. **Lid**: roll the roof fully forward (closed). Mark the exact position of the brackets and bolts
   first, then undo only the two 8 mm bolts per side. Leave every other fastener alone. Lift the
   lid out from above.
4. **Further in, if needed**: gutter / drip rail (align the keyway on the front clip by raising
   the lift arm), the transport bridge (2 cable bolts, 3 bolts per side), then the lift arms. On
   saloons don't remove the plastic side guides; they break (only coupés need it, FSM job 77-170).

Steps 2–4 are from JC220's W124 guide, which also covers cable lubrication with Gleitpaste
`A 001 989 46 51`, lift arm repair and the re-assembly and height adjustments
([HOW-TO: Complete W124 Sunroof Refurbishment, 500Eboard, 2017](https://www.500eboard.co/forums/threads/how-to-complete-w124-sunroof-refurbishment.11068/),
[reference](references/500eboard-w124-sunroof-refurbishment/README.md)). It puts getting the lid and lift arms
out at about 20 minutes. If the motor runs but the lid won't open, the whole
headliner and sunroof cassette have to come out.

For the side deflectors (above) only steps 1–3 are needed: the deflectors are riveted to the lid.

## Notes

- The panel comes off, the old crumbled deflectors are removed and the new ones riveted to the
  panel. A 1993 190E 2.3 owner had it done with `A 140 780 03 92`; a pair of genuine parts cost
  about $35 in 2014. 15 photos
  ([Люк помолодел, DRIVE2](https://www.drive2.ru/l/3733317/),
  [reference](references/drive2-lyuk-pomolodel/README.md)).
- The W124 tilt sunroof uses the same riveted rubber side cover. It can be bent aside a little;
  cutting it off to reach the lift mechanism is a last resort
  ([Reparatur Hubwinkel, mb124 archive](https://archiv.mb124.de/index.php?title=Reparatur_Hubwinkel),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20230325205612/https://archiv.mb124.de/index.php?title=Reparatur_Hubwinkel)).
- The same power tilt sunroof is used on W126 (1986–91), W201 (1987–93) and W124 (1986–95)
  ([Kent Bergsma, YouTube](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sR8kHMxiemw),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20250917161137/https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sR8kHMxiemw)).
