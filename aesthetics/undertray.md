# Engine undertray (planned)

**System:** body
**Status:** planned

Plastic panel under the engine. **This car has none fitted** (owner, 2026-10-06). It is missing
a factory part: this car's catalog (EPC 452) lists one, and US 1988 2.3 owners describe removing a
lower engine cover held by 6 × 8 mm-head bolts before an oil change
([PeachParts](http://www.peachparts.com/shopforum/tech-help/30701-oil-change-190e.html),
[reference](references/peachparts-oil-change-190e/README.md)).

The eBay listing that started this ("lower engine splash shield", open centre, "M-190E") cross-references
`A 201 524 12 30`.

Catalog group 52.030 "Engine compartment panelling, gasoline vehicles", via elcats

| Catalog | Front panel ("capsule") | `A 201 524 12 30` |
|---|---|---|

| 431, Europe to 09/88 | none | **yes, the only undertray** |
| 15C, Europe from 09/88 | `A 201 524 41 25` | yes, behind the capsule |
| 15D, US from 09/88 | `A 201 524 41 25` | not listed |

## Which panel for this car

BenzWorld member MTI lists the 2.3 panels by model year
([Belly-skid plate info](https://www.benzworld.org/threads/belly-skid-plate-info.1248935/),
[reference](references/bw-belly-skid-plate-info/README.md);
[Engine Splash Guard for 190E 2.3l](https://www.benzworld.org/threads/engine-splash-guard-for-190e-2-3l.1399000/),
[reference](references/bw-engine-splash-guard-23/README.md)):

| Model years | Panel | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| 1984–1985 | `A 201 524 12 30` | The open-centre panel (the eBay one) |
| **1986–1988** | **`A 201 520 60 23`** | **Matches this car's catalog (EPC 452 52.030 pos 5).** MTI adds that a front boot and a strut cover complete the set |
| 1991–1993 | `A 201 524 41 25` front, sides `A 201 520 85 23` (R) / `A 201 520 42 23` (L) | Facelift. EPC 452 lists the right side as `A 201 520 49 23` for this car |

So the factory part for this car is **`A 201 520 60 23`**, with the side parts it already lists
(`A 201 520 42 23` left, `A 201 520 49 23` right). `A 201 524 12 30` is the earlier European design;
a BenzWorld member says a Euro 2.3 panel should fit a US 2.3, while another warns the pre- and
post-facelift mounting points differ
([skid plate](https://www.benzworld.org/threads/skid-plate.1270278/),
[Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20180303121351/http://www.benzworld.org/forums/w201-190-class/1270278-skid-plate.html)).
*Not confirmed on this car.*

## Mounting `A 201 524 12 30`

- `A 201 524 12 30` was used with **both** the pre-facelift and the facelift bumpers (catalogs 431
  and 15C), so the bumper choice doesn't matter for it. It bolts to the side members and the
  engine compartment, not to the bumper.
- Mounting parts for `A 201 524 12 30` alone (catalog 431, pre-facelift Europe). Sizes come from
  aftermarket equivalents and supplier pages, not from the Mercedes catalog
  ([reference](references/undertray-fastener-specs/README.md)):

  | Part | Number | Qty | Pos | Size / spec |
  |---|---|---|---|---|
  | Spacer strut, panel to side member | `A 201 524 03 47` | 2 | 11 | Bent steel strap, about 80 g; ships in a 23 cm package (online-teile). No drawing dimensions found. |
  | Bolt, strut to side member | `N 000933 008145` | 2 | 14 | Hex bolt DIN 933, **M8 × 1.25 × 18 mm**, grade 8.8, zinc (febi 04859, DT 9.02022; SAMPA and AUGER list 20 mm) |
  | Nut | `N 913002 008004` | 2 | 20 | **M8 × 1.25 self-locking hex nut**, 13 mm spanner, DIN 980/985 (DT 9.34009, AIC 55724). Footnote 012: from ident no. 024 031764 |
  | Clip nut | `A 001 994 98 45` | 6 | 23 | Sheet metal clip nut for **4.8 mm** screws, 19 × 10 mm (Frankberg). Undercar shield clip |
  | Sheet metal screw | `A 201 990 05 36` | 6 | 26 | Combination screw **4.8 × 19 mm**, 8 mm hex head (WS8), 26 mm washer, black zinc (SWG) |
  | Rubber edge strip | `A 201 505 41 86` | 2 × 100 mm | 8 | Rubber strip on the panel edge, sold by the meter. Still listed by online-teile (2026-10-06) |

- The facelift catalog (15C) fixes the panel together with the front capsule and side parts, so
  its counts cover all three: 6 × `A 201 990 05 36`, 10 × clip nut `A 001 994 98 45`, 10 × bolt
  `A 201 990 11 36`, 2 × `N 304017 008016` (M8 × 16 hex bolt, ISO 4017), 2 × washer
  `N 000125 008443`, 2 × bolt `N 000933 008145` and 2 × nut `N 913002 008004` for the struts.
  `A 201 990 11 36`: no size found; it is also used on this car's side panels (EPC 452 52.030
  pos 50), so measure one there.
- US cars never got it from the factory, so check the side members for the spacer mounting points.
- To copy the facelift layout instead: front capsule `A 201 524 41 25` (15C lists it "only with
  A/C"; this car has A/C) plus the rear panel.
- **Dimensions:** not published by the catalog, the sellers or the forum threads checked
  (2026-10-06). Measure under the car, or ask the seller.
- w201.com members retrofitting `A 201 524 12 30` list the same parts: panel, 2 struts
  `A 201 524 03 47` (about €59 each at MB in 2025), 6 clips, 6 screws `A 201 990 05 36`, 2 bolts and
  2 nuts `N 913002 008004` (standard parts from a hardware store). They give the bolt as
  `000933 008745`; the catalog reads `N 000933 008145`
  ([thread](https://w201.com/index.php?thread/95414-motorschutz-motorabdeckung-motorverkleidung-unten-nur-welche/),
  [reference](references/w201com-motorschutz-welche/README.md)).
- Petrol cars with factory A/C and all diesels had the closed panel (no oil-pan opening); the others
  the open one or none (same w201.com thread). A W201 club thread disagrees on whether the A/C panel
  has the opening ([Motorunterbodenverkleidung](https://w201-ev.de/forum/thread/8359-motorunterbodenverkleidung/),
  [Wayback](https://web.archive.org/web/20160810194834/http://w201-ev.de/forum/index.php/Thread/8359-Motorunterbodenverkleidung/)).
- 4-cylinder cars hold the front of the panel with two spring-steel brackets (the struts); the 2.6
  bolts its panel (`A 201 524 16 30`) straight to its larger radiator cross member. The panel only
  keeps out dirt; steel skid plates are aftermarket only
  ([Unterfahrschutz beim 190E 2,6l](https://w201.com/index.php?thread/91717-unterfahrschutz-beim-190e-2-6l-6-zyl/),
  [reference](references/w201com-unterfahrschutz-26/README.md)).
- A separate gearbox panel exists. On a 2.3 it needs trimming around the exhaust, 2 holes drilled in
  the frame and heat wrap on the downpipes (w201.com thread above).
