# To buy: aesthetic parts

Cosmetic parts to replace or add, for looks rather than function. Part numbers come from EPC
catalog 452 (190 E 2.3 USA, 1988), via the private parts catalog (available on request).

## Engine bay

### Ignition cable cover (on the cylinder head cover)

EPC 15.060 Ignition System. Two long pieces clip together over the spark plug cables on top of the
cylinder head cover. A short end piece guides the cables and the breather line.

- [ ] **Ignition cable cover, top**: `A 102 159 11 40` (pos 176). The part you see with the hood open
- [ ] **Ignition cable holder, bottom**: `A 102 159 12 40` (pos 179). The base the cables sit in
- [ ] **End guide, cables and breather line**: `A 102 159 19 40` (pos 185)

The catalog names all three "bracket, ignition cables" and lists no replacement numbers.

### Air intake hose (to the oval air cleaner)

EPC 09.015 Air Cleaner. Ribbed hose into the inlet of the oval air cleaner housing
(`A 015 094 88 02`, confirmed on this car).

- [ ] **Hose**: **`A 102 094 56 82`** (pos 80), confirmed for this car. It connects the intake
  scoop next to the radiator to the air cleaner. Reproduction sold by
  [rpm-depot](https://rpm-depot.de/en/product/air-filter-intake-hose-m102-8v-mercedes-w201-190e-up-to-9-1988-a1020945682/)
  ([reference](references/rpm-depot-m102-intake-hose/README.md)), listed for M102.985 KE-Jetronic,
  up to 9/1988. The other hose at pos 80, `A 102 094 17 87`, is not the one for this car
- [ ] *Alternative:* [rpm-depot "Upgrade intake" kit](https://rpm-depot.de/en/product/upgrade-intake-mercedes-w201-190e-1-8-2-0-2-3-m102/)
  (aftermarket scoop, hose and fastening parts). The listing says it fits "M102 models with small
  radiator", so check that it fits this car first

## Body

### Seal at the rear of the engine bay (below the hood)

EPC 68.055 Insulation in engine compartment. U-channel edge seal that slips over the edge of the
panel at the rear of the engine bay, where the hood closes against the fire wall.

- [ ] **Seal, partition panel to fire wall**: `A 124 628 52 98` (pos 32), 1715 mm.
  **Order `A 210 628 00 98` instead.** A supplier search showed "use 210-628-00-98 instead"
  (the replacement doesn't appear in EPC 452)

### Sunroof side wind deflectors

EPC 78.075 Sliding/lifting roof plate (electric sunroof, option 412). Side covers riveted along the
left and right edges of the sunroof panel. They cover the side gaps when the roof is tilted up.
The catalog calls them "ornamental cover".

- [ ] **Side deflector, left**: `A 140 780 03 92` (pos 47). Replaces `A 126 780 07 92`
- [ ] **Side deflector, right**: `A 140 780 04 92` (pos 47). Replaces `A 126 780 08 92`
- [ ] **Rivets**: `N 910 001 00 3201` (pos 50), 4 needed

### Sacco side cladding, side skirts, facelift bumpers

Facelift (Sportline-style) wide side panels, side skirts and US facelift bumpers, with every part
number, the clips and a clip kit comparison: see [body/sacco-cladding.md](../body/sacco-cladding.md).

- [ ] Panels: 5 per side plus the side skirt and its end caps, in primer (paint before fitting)
- [ ] Clip kit (rpm-depot ART 002359 or TrabHan C3105) plus the side skirt hardware and tape
- [ ] Front bumper `A 201 880 15 70`, rear bumper `A 201 880 15 71` (US facelift, complete with cover)
- [ ] *Optional:* "sportline" badges `A 201 817 13 20` (2)

### Engine undertray, rear panel (under the engine/gearbox)

Black plastic panel with a large square opening, sold as "lower engine splash shield" for petrol
W201s. Sellers give interchange `A 201 524 12 30`.

- Facelift European catalog (15C, 52.030 "Engine compartment panelling, gasoline vehicles"):
  **`A 201 524 12 30`** is pos 17, the rear panel behind the front anti-noise capsule. It mounts
  with 2 spacers `A 201 524 03 47` (pos 32) bolted to the side members (`N 000933 008145` ×2,
  nut `N 913002 008004` ×2), 10 bolts `A 201 990 11 36` and clip nuts `A 001 994 98 45`
  ([reference](../body/references/elcats-w201-facelift-catalog/README.md)).
- **This car's catalog (EPC 452) doesn't list it**, and neither does the US facelift catalog (15D).
  US cars only have the front capsule (here `A 201 520 60 23`) and the two side parts. So it was
  never fitted to US cars; check the side members for the spacer mounting points before buying.
- **Dimensions:** not published by the catalog or the sellers checked (2026-10-06). Measure under
  the car, or ask the seller.

- [ ] Rear undertray `A 201 524 12 30` (aftermarket copies exist), plus spacers and bolts above
